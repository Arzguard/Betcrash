Responsible gaming

BetCrash is for adults aged 18 and over. Play for fun, set limits, and know where to get help.

⚠️
Gambling should be entertainment, not a way to make money. Only bet what you can afford to lose. If it stops being fun, stop and talk to someone — free help is listed below.

Warning signs

  • Spending more than you planned, or money you can't afford to lose
  • Chasing losses — betting again to win back what you lost
  • Borrowing money to gamble
  • Lying to family or friends about how much you gamble
  • Feeling anxious, irritable or restless when you're not gambling
  • Neglecting work, school or family because of gambling

If any of these sound familiar, you don't have to deal with it alone. The organisations below offer free, confidential support.

How BetCrash protects you

In place

Age & identity controls

18+ gate at entry, age confirmation at registration, and identity verification (national ID, date of birth, face match) before you can deposit or withdraw — as required by Kenyan gaming regulations. One account per ID number.

In place

No fabricated activity

Player counts and transaction history show only real activity. Until the game server is connected, every simulated round, balance and transaction is clearly labelled as a demo.

Coming with the game server

Deposit limits

Set a daily, weekly or monthly deposit limit from Account settings. Once set, it can't be raised for 7 days.

Coming with the game server

Time-out & self-exclusion

Take a break for 24 hours, 7 days or 30 days — or exclude yourself for 6 months or more. During self-exclusion, deposits and betting are blocked and your account is closed to play.

What you can do right now

  • Decide a budget before you play — and never exceed it
  • Set your own cash-out targets instead of "letting it ride"
  • Take regular breaks; don't play when stressed or upset
  • Never gamble with borrowed money or rent money
